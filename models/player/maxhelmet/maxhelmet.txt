

Maxhelmet - Skin

--------------------------------------------

Pretty straight forward . . . Max´s HEV-suit . . . helmet included.

Tao Xing found it in his kitchen under the sink,
while looking for his favorite chopsticks . . .

. . . apparently . . . Max left it on his last visit half a year ago.

When Max came to pick it up wearing his HEV-suit . . . Tao asked him if he could
make a duplicate of the whole thing . . .

. . . AND HERE IT IS . . . 

Max uses the full suit - in extreme hazardous environments and in teamwork . . .

. . . you´re welcome to do the same . . .


---------------------------------------------

Create a new folder in the valve/models/player - and name it "Maxhelmet" . . .
. . . place the MDL-file and maxhelmet-BMP-file in this folder . . .
. . . the maxlogo-BMP-file goes in the "logos" folder . . .

Start the game . . . choose "Maxhelmet" in the Multiplayer/Customize section . . .
and the "maxlogo" spraypaint . . .

. . . and you´re all set!

(. . . or type "model maxhelmet" in the console . . . while in the game).
